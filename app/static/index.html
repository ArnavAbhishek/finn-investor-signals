DATA TRANSPARENCY

How FINN updates

FINN checks public EOD market data and NSE filings automatically every four minutes while this page is open. Prices normally change only after a trading session; they do not move just because a timer ran.

Recheck sources clears the server's saved responses and attempts fresh requests. This is rate-limited to protect public endpoints.

Price charts: Yahoo Finance historical NSE data; an attributed TradingView widget may be offered if the host cannot retrieve history. Company filings: public NSE website (best effort).
